## Bedroom

Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: cinematic background plate for a short animated magic-lantern projection video, wide 16:9, 1536x864 or similar landscape.
Primary request: Recreate the intimate, melancholy atmosphere of the young narrator's bedroom at Combray in Marcel Proust's Swann's Way, late nineteenth-century provincial France, early evening just before dinner. This is an artistic interpretation, not a museum reconstruction.
Scene: seen from very low beside a child's wooden bed; a soft rumpled white pillow and edge of an ivory quilt in the lower right foreground. A genuine compact nineteenth-century tin-and-brass optical magic lantern, with horizontal lens barrel and oil-lamp housing/chimney, on a small wooden bedside table in lower left. Its lens faces diagonally toward the opposite wall, never toward camera. A faint cone of warm light and a few motes visible in the air.
Composition critical for later animation: broad mostly unobstructed pale warm plaster wall occupying middle and upper right two thirds, approximately x=38%-94%, y=12%-70%. A closed painted wooden bedroom door with a small brass knob is part of that back wall, around x=76%. Soft ivory curtain folds at extreme right of wall. The wall is very dim but readable, with a broad subtle warm oval wash from lantern. No projected story illustrations yet: leave the wall empty for later optical compositing. Keep architecture coherent and period scale believable. Bed occupies only bottom strip, lantern lower left, wall dominates.
Lighting: low-key, but retain shadow detail; amber wick glow, dim blue evening spill from an unseen window, smoky umber, antique gold, muted blue-black. No bright window, no electric lights.
Style: cinematic naturalism, tactile linen and worn plaster, restrained subtle film grain, soft vintage lens, sophisticated period-drama film frame. Quiet, mysterious, immersive. Camera fixed, 35mm equivalent, no fisheye.
No people in room, no text, no typography, no watermark, no illustration painted on wall, no modern equipment.

## Glass slide

Primary request: An authentic-looking hand-painted nineteenth-century magic-lantern illustration of the legend of Geneviève de Brabant as described in Proust: small triangular dark-green forest on the left, a softly glowing yellow-gold moor, and a cropped wing of a medieval golden castle on the far right, a very small Geneviève in a long pale dress and clearly blue girdle standing before it.
Composition: art sits within one broad horizontal elliptical area with irregular feathered perimeter fading entirely to pure RGB black in all outer margins. Castle occupies rightmost quarter of oval. Style: translucent jewel-like colored washes of pigment on old lantern-slide glass; simple charming folk drawing, flat medieval silhouettes and fine black ink outlines, visible transparent brushwork and warm optical halation. Restrained ruby/ochre/old gold/moss green/blue. Looks like a softly projected antique glass image rather than a modern painting or photograph. The artwork itself should glow, while everything outside it is perfectly black. The projection will be overlaid additively on another image; no room, no frame, no photographic glass rectangle, no projector, no screen, no lettering, no labels, no numbers.

Primary request: Golo from the legend of Geneviève de Brabant, as projected in Marcel Proust's bedroom scene. A pale melancholy medieval horseman with a distinctly RED cloak rides a pale cream horse toward the RIGHT in complete side profile. Both full horse and full rider visible, with generous empty margins. Horse in a modest trotting pose with one foreleg raised; not rearing, not galloping. Style: hand-painted nineteenth-century transparent magic-lantern glass pigments, fine black contours, simple naive folk illustration. Translucent ruby-red cloak, ochre harness, ivory horse, softly luminous color and faint optical halation, like light through stained glass. Same visual vocabulary as an antique illustrated legend.
Background: perfectly pure BLACK, RGB 0,0,0, everywhere outside the silhouette. No ground line, no ground shadow, no landscape, no castle, no lantern apparatus, no room, no border, no text. It is a compositing element, not a finished poster. Keep all of the horse's legs, ears, tail and cloak comfortably inside the canvas.
